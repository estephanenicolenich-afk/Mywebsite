Explore essential legal frameworks, navigate ethical dilemmas like a professional IT expert, and sign your digital student pledge!

1. Key Cyber Legislation & Data Privacy

Data Privacy Act (DPA)

Protects individual personal information in information and communications systems in government and the private sector, mandating consent and secure data handling.

Anti-Cybercrime Act

Criminalizes illegal access, data interference, identity theft, cybersex, and cyberbullying with stiff legal penalties to ensure internet safety.

Digital Copyright & Ethics

Governs intellectual property online, prohibiting software piracy, unauthorized asset redistribution, and intellectual theft of digital creations.
